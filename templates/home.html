How it works

  1. 1

    Choose

    New starters create an account and pick the laptop, monitor, phone and accessories they prefer from what is in stock.

  2. 2

    Approve

    A manager checks each request against the person's role and approves it, or rejects it with a reason.

  3. 3

    Track and return

    Every item's condition and holder is recorded. When someone leaves, the handback is logged and the item goes back into stock or for repair.
